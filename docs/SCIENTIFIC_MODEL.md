# Scientific model, conventions, and diagnostic scope

## 1. End-to-end statistical pipeline

1. **Full covariance preparation & SN quality pre-filter**: Read the 130-system strong-lens catalog (`codeanddata/130sgls2.CSV`) and 1820-row DES-Dovekie Hubble diagram (`DES-Dovekie_HD.csv`). Reconstruct the full $1820 \times 1820$ statistical-plus-systematic covariance matrix $C_{\rm tot}$ from the packed precision matrix (`covtot_inv_000.npz`) via Cholesky solve. Retain rows with $\sqrt{C_{{\rm tot},jj}} \le 0.30$ mag (1641 eligible rows), preserving original parent catalog indices (`0..1819`).
2. **Global two-stage MILP assignment (`CosmoMatcher` v1.4.3)**: Solve Stage 1 to maximize the number of complete lens--SN--SN triplets ($K = 88$) subject to joint lens/source row non-reuse (`unique_by=row`, `tie_break=none`) and candidate relative radial-distance tolerance $\epsilon = 0.05$ in flat $\Omega_m^{\rm fid} = 0.3$; solve Stage 2 at fixed $K = 88$ to minimize total squared relative distance mismatch to zero MIP optimality gap, yielding 176 distinct DES rows.
3. **Selected covariance & finite-redshift mismatch envelope**: Extract the exact $176 \times 176$ submatrix $C_{\rm sub}$ (including lens--lens, source--source, and lens--source cross-blocks) and add the squared fiducial modulus offset $b_{ia}^2$ on the diagonal as an rms mismatch envelope ($C_m = C_{\rm sub} + \operatorname{diag}(b_{ia}^2)$).
4. **FLRW sum rule & spherical Jeans power-law dynamics**: Convert latent distance moduli $\boldsymbol m = (\boldsymbol\mu_l, \boldsymbol\mu_s)$ to dimensionless transverse comoving distances $d_a = (H_0 / c) D_L(z_a) / (1 + z_a)$ at fixed catalog normalization $H_0 = 70\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$, evaluate $R = \sqrt{1 + \Omega_k d_l^2} - (d_l / d_s)\sqrt{1 + \Omega_k d_s^2}$, and predict circular-aperture velocity dispersions $\sigma_{\rm th}$ under $\gamma_i = \gamma_0 + \gamma_z z_{l,i}$, individual light slopes $\delta_i$, global anisotropy $\beta$, and fractional intrinsic scatter $f_{\rm int}$. States violating $1 + \Omega_k d_a^2 > 0$, $R > 0$, $1 < \xi_i < 3$, $\xi_i > 2\beta$, $1.05 < \gamma_i < 2.95$, or $\sigma_{{\rm th},i}^2 > 0$ are strictly rejected.
5. **Hierarchical nonlinear MCMC**: Update $\Theta = (\Omega_k, \gamma_0, \gamma_z, \beta, f_{\rm int})$ via adaptive Metropolis (freezing proposal adaptation at burn-in) and update $\boldsymbol m$ via elliptical slice sampling against $\mathcal N(\boldsymbol m; \boldsymbol m_{\rm obs}, C_m)$. Linearized comparison runs project $C_m$ analytically via exact derivatives $(J_l, J_s)$ and sample with `emcee`.

## 2. Priors and normalization conventions

* **Distance normalization**: $H_0 = 70\ \mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$ is fixed to the DES-Dovekie catalog convention rather than sampled; under a general Hubble scale or zero-point offset $a$, the constrained catalog combination is $\mathcal K_\mu = \Omega_k (H_0 / 70)^2 10^{0.4a}$.
* **Primary parameter bounds**: $\Omega_k \in (-0.09405370006986057, 2.0)$, $\gamma_0 \in (1.1, 2.8)$, $\gamma_z \in (-0.5, 0.5)$, $\beta \in (-0.8, 0.8)$, and $f_{\rm int} \in (0.0, 0.3)$ (internal code parameter `q` denotes $\Omega_k$ in this fixed normalization).
* **CMB geometric consistency bound**: $D_A(0, 1090) = 12.8 \pm 0.07$ Mpc sets $d_* = (1 + 1090)(70 / c) \times 12.8$ and $\Omega_{k,\min} = -1 / d_*^2 = -0.0940537$. The `soft07` run propagates the $0.07$-Mpc uncertainty via a Gaussian-CDF geometric weight over $\Omega_k < 0$.

## 3. Sensitivity, residual, and simulation diagnostics

* **Physical & rematching sensitivities**: Cover soft CMB weight, illustrative dynamical noise, Gaussian $\beta$ prior, coherent $\delta_i \pm 0.05$ shifts, wide evolution prior $\gamma_z \in (-1, 1)$, alternative SN uncertainty cuts ($< 0.25$, $< 0.35$ mag), matching tolerances ($\epsilon = 0.03$, $0.07$), and curvature upper bound $\Omega_k < 5$.
* **Exact-redshift reconstructions**: Compare deterministic signed fiducial transfer (with and without $b_{ia}^2$ envelope) and full-covariance DES GP reconstructions (log-distance and flat-$\Lambda$CDM prior means) on the exact same 88 lenses and 176 DES rows, alongside separate linearized Union3 GP comparisons (`Same-88` and `119` covered lenses); the four same-DES checks marginalize distances nonlinearly.
* **Posterior-predictive velocity residuals**: Global squared velocity residuals match well ($p_{\rm pp} = 0.435$), whereas the source-redshift residual projection exhibits a persistent negative score ($p_{\rm pp} = 0.0012$–$0.0025$ raw; $0.0039$–$0.0121$ partial) across the tested discrete and exact-redshift treatments. These treatments do not remove the ordered residual, but they do not uniquely separate lens-population selection, dynamics, line-of-sight structure, or calibration causes.
* **Fixed-design injection--recovery simulations**: Six 60,000-step four-chain realizations at $\Omega_k^{\rm true} = 0$ have zero inside all six central $95.45\%$ intervals and outside all six central $68.27\%$ intervals, with positive 1D marginal medians ($0.504$–$0.712$). This behavior is compatible with weak identification and marginal-volume effects on the asymmetric prior domain. The six source-redshift checks give $p_{\rm pp}=0.384$--$0.672$; these six fixed-design realizations characterize posterior geometry on the observed lens configuration and show no trend comparable to the observed one in these six fixed-design realizations of the assumed generating model.

The effective $\gamma(z_l)$ relation is conditional on the adopted population model and evolution prior. Widening $\gamma_z$ to $[-1,1]$ moves its median to $-0.876$ and the curvature median to $0.209$; the primary posterior has 33.61% mass within 0.05 of the lower evolution bound. Physical time evolution and survey selection have not been separated.
